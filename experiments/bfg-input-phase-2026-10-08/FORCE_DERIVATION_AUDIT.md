# Force-law derivation: regular-factor obstruction for the driven scalar kernel
This is an actual proof obligation addressed, not a promise of a future derivation. It narrows the scalar route; it is not an impossibility result for the full matrix BFG or every extension.

## Statement and premises
Use Dynamic Order section88.2/Theorem7: constant0<d<3/4, full-persistence scalar sector, K,F inherited unchanged, y+=f(y+d), positive fixed y*. Work at an interior F>0. For x=(K,F,y), the derivative of U at x* is D=diag(1,1,lambda), lambda=f'(y*+d), 0<=lambda<=16/27. Scalar formula and neutral K,F inheritance are identified in the active source; no old paper needed.
Additional proposed physical target is the passive sine pendulum with fixed a>0,b>0 and calibrated event duration h>0. Let M=exp(hJ), J=[[0,1],[-a,-b]], the derivative of the physical time-h map at downward rest. Suppose a C1 projection Pi sends x* to rest and satisfies the section89 equation(161) as a time-h semiconjugacy in a neighborhood, including K,F perturbations, with fixed target parameters. Claim: its differential at x* cannot have rank2.

## Proof
Differentiate Pi U=Phi_h Pi at the fixed point: B D=M B, B=D Pi(x*), a2x3 matrix. The eigenvalues of J have negative real part for a,b>0: either both negative real or a complex conjugate pair with real part-b/2. Thus all eigenvalues of M have modulus strictly below1, and M-I is invertible. Write B=[v_K,v_F,v_y]. The first two columns give (M-I)v_K=(M-I)v_F=0, hence v_K=v_F=0. The last gives (M-lambda I)v_y=0. At most this single column is nonzero, so rank B<=1. This contradicts any regular rank2 physical-state bridge.
For underdamped motion and sin(h sqrt(a-b²/4))!=0, M has nonreal eigenvalues, whereas lambda is real: M-lambda I is also invertible and B=0. At a resonant h the stronger B=0 claim may fail, but rank2 remains impossible. Overdamped motion can align one eigenvalue with lambda and yield rank1; this does not establish the full pendulum phase space.

## Limits and constructive consequence
A rank-deficient/singular readout is not ruled out; an orbit-specific readout, added event counter, extra evolving physical state or higher-dimensional matrix carrier is outside the premises. The existing calibrated counter/flow representations use exactly these extra structures and therefore do not violate this theorem. At b=0 a resonant physical map may have eigenvalue1; the passive attracting premise is essential. This result does not select sine rather than other force laws, identify gravity, or refute every BFG realization.
To pursue a genuinely internal force derivation, the next candidate must supply at least two observable evolving directions and demonstrate compatible tangent dynamics, not insert the desired generator into a scalar readout. For matrix candidates, compute the full regular-chain derivative with rank/gap assumptions from the active paper, then test a proposed force/observable intertwining and independent force-selection constraints. Extra torque coupling remains explicitly declared. No altered holdout or invented mechanical constants are needed to prove this obstruction.
