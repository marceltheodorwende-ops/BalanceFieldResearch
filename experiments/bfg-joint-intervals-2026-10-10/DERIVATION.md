# P73 — Joint interval certificates and nonlinear family envelope

## Three-position exact fixed-time certificate
At true times t_-<t_0<t_+, hL=t_0-t_-, hR=t_+-t_0, slopes sL=(Q0-Q-)/hL and sR=(Q+-Q0)/hR. For |Q''|<=A, integrate |W(t)-W(u)|<=A(t-u) over u∈[t_-,t0],t∈[t0,t_+] to obtain |sR-sL|<=A(hL+hR)/2. For exact known times, r=(q+-q0)/hR-(q0-q-)/hL has uncertainty radius 2e(1/hL+1/hR). Therefore A>=2 max(0,|r|-2e(1/hL+1/hR))/(hL+hR). This lower bound is SHARP for a single position triple: choose endpoints/center in their closed measurement boxes to minimize |r|, then the interpolating quadratic has constant acceleration 2r/(hL+hR) and realizes those three positions. This proves only triple feasibility; choosing independent quadratics per triple need not give one global trajectory or match velocity/force.

For uncertain times use P72 mean-slope outer intervals IL,IR obtained from four endpoint ratios with numerator displacement±2e and denominator reported separation±2eta. Require both reported separations>2eta. Let gap(IL,IR)=max(0,loL-hiR,loR-hiL). Actual duration t_+-t_-<=reported total H+2eta (center timing cancels). Thus Apos=2gap/(H+2eta) is a rigorous lower bound. Independent slope boxes ignore shared-center correlation, so this bound can be looser than the sharp fixed-time expression; do not call it exact uncertain-time feasibility.

## Joint instantaneous-velocity certificate
Let Ispan be the outer mean velocity interval from the two outside angle samples. If reports are symmetric, true center differs from actual temporal midpoint by at most2eta. P72's integral gives |W(mid)-mean|<=A h/4. Hence |W(t0^true)-mean|<=A[(H+2eta)/4+2eta]. Define Iv=[w0-ew,w0+ew]. Then Avel=gap(Iv,Ispan)/[(H+2eta)/4+2eta] is necessary. Take Ajoint=max(Apos,Avel). This imposes a declared instantaneous velocity meaning; if exported velocity is filtered/estimated by a different law the certificate's measurement conjunction is inapplicable, not evidence of bad physics. No derivative stencil is inferred.

Widening e,ew or eta widens the slope/velocity outer intervals and increases positive duration/center denominators, so neither lower bound increases. Invalid ordering has no certificate. A constant common offset cancels position differences, but energy/force require a calibrated physical zero. Winding/reset/gain errors need an explicitly extended observation family. Smaller spacing can amplify position uncertainty; larger spacing loses instantaneous detail. All multiscale windows describe time aggregation, not fractality.

## Why local passage is not global feasibility
For exact reports t=0,1,2,3 and positions0,0,1,1, both overlapping triples have sharp minimum A=1. Yet no C² trajectory with |Q''|<=1 realizes all four. The first triple saturates sR-sL=1, forcing Q''=1 on(0,2) by equality in the integrated Lipschitz inequality. The second saturates -1, forcing Q''=-1 on(1,3). They contradict on(1,2). Therefore local triple certificates can all pass while the coherent path set is empty. Instrument boxes/times do not remove the need for a global trajectory/force check. This explicit counterexample closes the scope of P73's no-rejection finding.
