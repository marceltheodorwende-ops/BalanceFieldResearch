# Exploratory stability protocol — fixed before this evaluation

Scope: previously calibrated sine-flow realization and analytic phase-corrected observable, attempt1 only (15 files). No refitting, no holdout access. Source and coefficients remain the pinned versions from the published packages. Evaluate all0.1-second-spaced forecast starts in seconds30–60 at horizons0.1,1.6,6.4s. Mean per-record RMSE divided by target RMS, separately angle and velocity; preserve all records and stress scenarios. Longer predictions overlap.

One-factor-at-a-time deterministic perturbations: initial angle plus/minus(delta_theta=pi/2000+0.0005rad, illustrative half manufacturer encoder step plus half CSV rounding step); initial velocity plus/minus0.0005rad/s (CSV rounding only, not full uncertainty); gravity coefficient a plus/minus1%; viscous coefficient b plus/minus1%; seconds-per-event duration plus/minus1%. Include nominal. Ranges are declared scenarios, not calibrated confidence intervals or a joint uncertainty box. No tuning from resulting scores. Physical targets remain unchanged, and each forecast uses only its perturbed initial state. Perturbations are sensitivity controls on real-data forecasts, not new measurements.

Decision/report rule: publish every scenario, worst scenario for each model/metric/horizon, change from nominal, and per-record values. No claim of absolute stability or statistical superiority. Mathematical dynamical stability requires separate premises. Count initial states satisfying |theta|<pi and E/a = w²/(2a)+1-cos(theta)<2, with b>0; this supplies a conditional basin certificate for nominal sine dynamics, not a prediction-error guarantee. Compare the two representations under identical disturbances. Tests independently check the sine integrator and phase observable. Max45minutes per GitHub reproduction, at most one empirical run active.
