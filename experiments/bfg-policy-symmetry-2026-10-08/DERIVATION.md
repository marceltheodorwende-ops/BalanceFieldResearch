## Proof
If L†Xi L=Xi, covariance gives D(Xi,r)=D(L†Xi L,r)=L†D(Xi,r)L. Thus D is in the commutant of the stabilizer. If every input matrix is diagonal, every diagonal unitary L=diag(exp(i phi_j)) stabilizes it. Entry ij of D becomes exp(i(phi_j-phi_i))D_ij. Vary phases independently; for i≠j invariance implies D_ij=0. No simplicity assumption needed for this diagonal conclusion. If all matrices are scalar, the stabilizer is U(n), whose commutant is scalar multiples of identity (diagonal phase argument followed by permutations). At a common degenerate block where all input operators are scalar, D restricted to that block must be scalar and cross terms to other independently phased blocks vanish.

Under such diagonal preparation with 0<Y+D<I,P=I, diagonal K,F,W, canonical channels and natural-frame R are diagonal; K successor is diagonal and formation/witness unchanged. Thus the commuting stratum remains commuting by induction, conditional on domain and full-persistence gates. Canonical SVD coordinates may rotate degenerate blocks; this is a quotient statement, not fixed arbitrary eigenvector selection. Nonterminality follows positive loads/full rank/empty complement. This is NOT a guarantee of positive reserves or physical oscillation.

## Compatible alternative and limits

An explicit EXTRA policy D_eta=eta i[Y,K]/kappa with kappa>0 in K's formation units and real dimensionless eta is Hermitian, dimensionless and covariant. For ||D_eta||<min(lambda_min Y,1-lambda_max Y), Weyl inequalities guarantee prepared domain. It vanishes on commuting Y,K. Both signs of eta have equal operator-norm cost in P49 but differ as preparations; quotient symmetry and resource accounting do not select the sign or coefficient. This is an additional input rule, not a canonical force. Controls choose kappa=1 abstract matched unit, not a calibrated physical constant.

For Y=diag(.2,.7),K=[[0,.2],[.2,0]],eta=.01, D has imaginary offdiagonal entries of magnitude .001. Both signs are admissible. The two canonical tuples related by a diagonal sign change have gauge-equivalent sign conventions when the rest is transformed too: no physical handedness is inferred from matrix-entry signs.
