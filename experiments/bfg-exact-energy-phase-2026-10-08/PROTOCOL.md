# Exact energy–phase closure: development protocol
Frozen before this package's evaluation. No new fitting or model selection.
Use only the fifteen attempt-1 recordings and already published viscous coefficients fitted on seconds 0–30. Forecast from seconds 30–60 at horizons 0.1, 1.6, 6.4 seconds, origins every ten samples. Compare exact energy–phase chart, full sine mechanical dynamics and persistence on identical origins; report per-record target-RMS normalized angle/velocity errors, coverage and numerical discrepancy. Reject rather than silently clip initial states outside the libration chart; record every exclusion. Holdout attempts 2/3 remain unopened.
Mathematical controls: round trips; independent DOP853 integration in Cartesian and energy–phase coordinates; undamped energy conservation; rest and domain rejection. Numerical tolerances for independent flow agreement: 1e-8 absolute; RK4 chart-versus-Cartesian comparison 1e-5 absolute at step <=0.005 seconds. Failure is retained, not repaired by changing these thresholds.
Stability claim: forward invariant libration-energy well, positivity over finite time, bounded observable Jacobian away from separatrix. No absolute predictive stability or independent force-law derivation. Phase becomes ill-conditioned near rest; explicit rest handling is required. No empirical superiority is expected from an exact coordinate change.
