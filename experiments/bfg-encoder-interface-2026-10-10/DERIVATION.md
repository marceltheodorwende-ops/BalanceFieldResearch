## Conditional bounded observation
Let Q(t) be unwrapped angle in rad, b a known offset, and z_i the rounded exported sample of nearest uniform quantization of Q(t_i)-b plus bounded analog error epsilon. If export error <=r, then |Q(t_i)-(z_i+b)|<=e=delta/2+epsilon+r. Triangle inequality proves this outer enclosure; it need not be sharp after two quantizers. Truncation instead of nearest rounding requires delta, not delta/2. Unknown b needs a separate interval; unknown winding introduces 2pi integer alternatives. None of these hypotheses is inferred from decimal precision.

For h=t_1-t_0>0, with reported separation H and endpoint timing error <=eta, h in [H-2eta,H+2eta]. Set d=z_1-z_0; displacement belongs to [d-2e,d+2e]. If H>2eta, mean velocity lies in the min/max of the four ratios (d±2e)/(H±2eta). This follows because a linear-fractional expression over a rectangle with positive denominator reaches extrema at vertices. It is an outer interval, not a probability/confidence interval. If H<=2eta, positive duration has no lower bound and generally no finite velocity bound; coincident/reversed times are inadmissible. Offset cancels only when the same offset applies at both endpoints.

For an EXTRA independent |Q''|<=A throughout the interval, midpoint velocity differs from average velocity by <=Ah/4: integrate |Q'(t)-Q'(mid)|<=A|t-mid| and divide by h. Thus enlarge the mean interval by A(H+2eta)/4. For endpoint velocity use Ah/2. A is in rad/s²; eta seconds, e radians, ratios rad/s. The bound is a deterministic worst-case statement, not an actual device uncertainty estimate. Choosing longer h reduces quantization amplification but increases the derivative-bound term. Neither alone validates exported velocity.

## Exact factor failure, including a canonical BFG example
Uniform quantization C_delta(Q)=floor(Q/delta+1/2) is constant inside bins: derivative rank zero there and discontinuous on boundaries. It is not a rank-two smooth state chart. Even observing exact W alongside C_delta(Q) fails autonomous closure for the EXTRA free-motion flow Qdot=W,Wdot=0. Take Q±=±delta/4 and W=delta/(2h). Initial outputs coincide, while after h they are counts0 and1. Therefore fiber closure fails independently of noise.

A concrete canonical example uses Dynamic Order II88.1(153)–(154), full-persistence scalar state n=P=rhoW=1,rhoF=1,K=0,y in (0,1). Readout C_.01(y) (dimensionless, explicitly NOT physical angle) gives identical count5 for y=13/250 and27/500. Their canonical successors f(y)=2y²/[(1+y)²(1+y²)] lie strictly below/above .005 and have counts0/1. All loads are positive, seed complement empty; no terminal ambiguity. Hence a plausible coarse readout can fail the actual canonical fiber condition. This does not rule out other BFG carriers or valid physical projections.

If a smooth physical chart Pi=(Q,W) and positive C were independently established, it still must satisfy Pi(Ux)=phi_F^{C(x)}Pi(x). Differentiating gives DPi(Ux)DU(x)=Dphi_F^C(Pi x)DPi(x)+F(Pi(Ux))DC(x). Quantization cannot inherit this as an invertible tangent chart. Interval observations instead produce a set of latent states; propagating {Ux:x in S} and retaining compatible measurement bins is an epistemic outer construction, not a changed or controlled canonical kernel. Terminal successors remain explicit alternatives; admissibility gates and clock bounds must be checked for every retained state for an all-state certificate.

## Concrete force ambiguity and its scope
For known delta>0 choose B>0,a_n=delta/[8(1+n)], Omega_n=Bn/delta, Q_n(t)=a_n cos(Omega_n t). All times have count0; Q_0(t)=0 also has count0. |Q_n'|<B/8, while |Q_n''(0)|=B²n²/[8delta(1+n)] diverges. Each is a smooth conservative harmonic trajectory Q''=-Omega_n²Q with its own stiffness, contrasting the stationary preparation. Thus even an entire ideal quantized-angle history, plus a finite speed bound, does not identify acceleration or force over this unrestricted plant class. For positive specified inertia J, torque is JQ'' and likewise unbounded. This uses external mechanics as a countermodel; it asserts neither actual apparatus dynamics nor BFG realizability of every rival. A independently fixed force family, bandwidth/acceleration bound or independent high-resolution channel can restrict it. Actual exported velocity could distinguish some trajectories if its estimator/accuracy were certified; no such law is assumed here.
