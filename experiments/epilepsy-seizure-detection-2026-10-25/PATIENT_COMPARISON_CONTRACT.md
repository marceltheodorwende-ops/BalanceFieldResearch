# EP01 paired patient aggregation and resampling kernel

Status: bounded exact-statistic/resampling construction verified; inferential protocol and EP01 remain OPEN. Statistical/evaluation definitions, not BFG equations or biological laws. No real detector comparison exists.

Pre-implementation contract: two methods require the same complete expected record set and event convention. First aggregate all repeated sessions by validated person identity, then resample whole people with identical draws for both methods. Preserve zero-event people and all recorded hours; no record-level pseudo-replication or silently discarded undefined draws. No confidence interval or clinical decision until the inferential rule and precision are fully specified.

For person p let T_p be full recorded seconds, E_p original events, D_ap,D_bp timely detected original events and F_ap,F_bp false activations. Pooled differences are delta_S=sum(D_ap-D_bp)/sum(E_p) when sum(E_p)>0, and delta_F=86400*sum(F_ap-F_bp)/sum(T_p). These are event-weighted sensitivity and time-weighted false-activation-rate estimands, not equal-person mean estimands; choosing the primary estimand remains a protocol decision. Counts/times are summed, not per-record ratios averaged. General additivity proves invariance to splitting one person's recording while preserving its annotations/decisions and exposure; it does not justify changing alarm behavior at file boundaries. Actual patient aliases must come from the audited manifest; strings alone do not prove identity.

aggregate_pairs gates both methods through coverage_report, verifies a common deadline and combines records by person. paired_resamples consumes that verified patient table and explicit n-index draws. Each draw applies identical patient multiplicities to BOTH methods, preserving within-person session dependence and paired comparison. Exact rational statistics avoid arithmetic rounding. A draw containing only zero-event people yields undefined sensitivity and is retained/reported; its hours still contribute to false-alarm rates. No conditional deletion or automatic replacement draw. Explicit draws make RNG/resampling policy visible; no RNG count/seed or percentile convention is yet selected.

Scope: verified table from aggregate_pairs is required; standalone arbitrary tables are not authenticated. Manifest/receipt checks do not prove upstream score provenance or equal tuning budgets, QC, threshold policy or clinical annotation validity. Scorer deadline agreement is necessary, not sufficient, for fair comparison. A paired table is not independent confirmation.

One-person obstruction: n=1 forces every size-n resample to select that same person. Every replicate statistic equals its point statistic regardless of unknown between-patient variation. A zero-width empirical distribution therefore supplies no information about population generalization. Kernel rejects fewer than two distinct aggregated people. n>=2 merely allows resampling; it is NOT a sufficient sample size or precision certificate. Even many observed homogeneous people may give a degenerate bootstrap distribution. Independent/exchangeable patient sampling and transport to the target population require substantive justification; ordinary bootstrap coverage is not guaranteed for sparse events, small samples or boundary sensitivities.

Checks:8 control groups with repeated-session aggregation, independently enumerated four two-person draws, zero-event draw retention, hand-computed false-rate difference, identical-method cancellation,5 invalid resampling cases and incomplete-rival refusal. All synthetic. Existing cohort/record-score controls rerun and compilation checked. No new subject/sample/annotation access. Remaining uncertainty obligations OPEN: exact estimand, cluster-resampling scheme/replications/seed, interval convention, handling undefined replicates, paired inference, noninferiority margin, power/precision/design effect and final decision rule before holdout. Do not manufacture an interval from the current one-person real metadata audit. No data-dependent selection of the interval method after test.
