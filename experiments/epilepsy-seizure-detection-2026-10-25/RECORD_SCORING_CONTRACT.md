# EP01 record receipt to timely-event scoring adapter

Status: bounded evaluation integration verified, EP01 OPEN; no detector, clinical result or confirmation freeze. Uses the existing supplementary timely endpoint, not a replacement for pinned SzCORE. Protocol conventions are not biological or BFG laws.

Pre-implementation acceptance: explicitly equal recording identities for source annotations, masks and decisions; full original reference count and recorded-time denominator; alarm activation at sample-END time, ready output and declared evidence index no later than activation; malformed/ambiguous timestamps reject. No imputed decisions, hidden suppression, threshold tuning, tolerance or merging.

record_scoring.score_record first invokes the full-mask availability audit, then validates each supplied decision receipt {onset_sample_end,offset_sample_end,evidence_sample_end}. Indices are integers with1<=evidence<=onset<offset<=n. The activation's output ready[onset-1] must be true. Seconds are onset/f and offset/f, never(onset-1)/f. Original intervals remain unchanged. The existing timely scorer checks interval order/overlap/adjacency and matches with its documented deadline convention. Full duration and all source events, including fully unavailable events, are passed unchanged; all-unavailable/no-decision events remain misses. Alarm offset and held-alarm behavior during subsequent invalid output are supplied by the detector, not silently repaired by this adapter; final QC/hold/refractory policy remains OPEN.

Exact-to-float boundary: timely currently uses finite float seconds. The adapter requires Fraction.from_float(float(t))==t for every supplied time. Nonrepresentable rational boundaries fail rather than silently changing event membership. Integer annotations and256Hz sample-end times are exactly representable in the practical record range. This does not generalize to arbitrary rates or precision; a future exact scorer would require separate review. Undefined sensitivity on zero-event records remains undefined, with their full hours retained for false-alarm accounting.

Proof scope: sample-end inequality verifies declared evidence has no future index, not actual model causality or provenance. Matching strings detect accidental cross-record joins but do not cryptographically authenticate identity, mask origin, preprocessing or feature information use. A dishonest/miscomputed evidence receipt can pass. Independent detector information-flow verification, source/path/hash integration and full failure accounting remain mandatory before confirmation. The adapter does not assert that a ready output demonstrates calibrated clinical signal quality.

Checks:7 groups including9 invalid identity/future-evidence/unready/boundary/rounding cases, duplicate-alarm rejection, all-unavailable misses, unchanged full-time false-alarm rates, zero-deadline counterexample to onset backdating, and24 independently expected integer activation/deadline cases. Synthetic only; no EEG decisions generated. Prior availability/causal-stream/timely controls rerun, compilation verified. Original threshold/deadline candidates remain design choices, not clinical standards or finalized criteria.

Next dependency OPEN: failed/missing-record cohort receipts and patient aggregation without denominator loss; detector provenance and QC/filter/refractory settings; other source dialects/full acquisition coverage; BFG measurement/preparation/closure, strong rivals, tuning/patient uncertainty/precision and complete publication freeze.
