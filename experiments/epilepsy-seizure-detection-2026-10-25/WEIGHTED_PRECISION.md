## Statement and target
Fix both trained detectors and their thresholds independently of the evaluated cohort. Condition on the complete vector C of per-person original event counts E_p and full recorded durations T_p. Assume paired per-person detector outcomes are conditionally independent across actual people given C and the fixed detectors. This is NOT established by the available metadata and can fail under shared sites/dependence, selection or outcome-driven model choice. All repeated sessions of a person must first be aggregated. Complete matched scoring/source receipts remain required.

If sum E_p>0, define w_p=E_p/sum E. For E_p>0 let X_p=(D_ap-D_bp)/E_p in[-1,1]. Zero-event people have weight0 and need no defined X_p; they remain in all time/false-alarm reporting. The implemented pooled statistic is exactly sum w_p X_p. Its conditional target is theta(C)=sum w_p E[X_p|C], not an unconditional target-population ratio or automatically the same clinical estimand across cohorts. Conditioning makes the weights fixed; it does not prove independence, representative sampling, correct clinical labels or transportability. If sum E_p=0 sensitivity and this bound are undefined, not0 or1.

## Proof
The centered bounded-variable log-MGF argument in PRECISION_FEASIBILITY.md yields E[exp(t*w_p*(X_p-E[X_p|C]))|C]<=exp(t^2*w_p^2/2). Conditional independence multiplies these expectations, giving log-MGF<=t^2*q/2 for q=sum w_p^2>0. Markov optimization at t=r/q gives either-tail bound exp(-r^2/(2q)); union bound yields P(|sum wX-theta(C)|>=r|C)<=2exp(-r^2/(2q)). Hence r=sqrt(2q log(2/alpha)) is a conservative conditional radius. It remains valid unconditionally for the RANDOM conditional target theta(C) if the conditional premises hold almost surely; this does not establish coverage for a different fixed population target. Intersect the resulting interval with[-1,1]. No lower bound on achievable precision or actual power is claimed.

Writing n_weight=1/q, Cauchy-Schwarz gives(sum w)^2<=m sum w^2 and therefore n_weight<=m, the number of event-positive people. Since sum w^2<=sum w=1, n_weight>=1. Equality m requires equal positive weights; dominance by one person makes n_weight approach1. This is only a convenient concentration denominator, not proof of equivalent independent observations. Multiplying every E_p by a common constant leaves normalized weights, q and radius unchanged. More events with the same clustering do not create independent people.

For pooled false rates define v_p=T_p/sum T and X_p=86400*(F_ap-F_bp)/T_p. Under the common sample-clock bound|X_p|<=B=86400*f, the same proof gives r_F=B*sqrt(2log(2/alpha)*sum v_p^2). With varying known B_p use sqrt(2log(2/alpha)*sum(v_p*B_p)^2). No new suppression, artificial alarm cap or biological tail law is imposed. These operational bounds remain extremely loose. Duration weights and conditional rate targets require the same care as event weights.
