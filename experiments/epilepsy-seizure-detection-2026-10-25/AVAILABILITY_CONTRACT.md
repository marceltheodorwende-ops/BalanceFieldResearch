# EP01 availability/annotation duration join

Status: bounded accounting primitive verified; EP01 OPEN, not a detector or confirmatory freeze. Uses exact record-local annotations and complete masks from a single RecordStream execution. Evaluation definitions only; no physiological/BFG law or fitted repair term.

Contract fixed before implementation: invalid samples, warmup and events must not vanish from time or sensitivity denominators; incomplete/contradictory masks reject. Input duration/rate/original intervals pass recording_time.reconcile. Boolean masks contain exactly T*f samples; ready and invalid cannot both hold. Within the current RecordStream semantics every noninvalid, nonready cell is warmup. Other future abstention reasons require explicit extension, not relabelling as warmup. This interface trusts the caller's record identity and mask provenance; it does not authenticate mask/path alignment or recover missing samples. Failed/truncated records require a separate explicit failed-record receipt, not an invented complete mask.

Proof: cells C_i=[i/f,(i+1)/f) partition[0,T). Ready R, invalid I and remaining warmup W partition cells, hence T=|R|/f+|I|/f+|W|/f. For each original event[a,b), sum exact intersection lengths over ready cells; unavailable overlap is b-a minus that sum. Disjoint partition gives nonnegative additivity, including annotations off the sample grid. No rounding/clipping/merging or reference-event deletion. Original count and full T remain the sensitivity and false-alarm-rate denominator inputs. Those outputs are accounting inputs, not actual computed sensitivity or alarm rates.

Timing distinction: ready status for cell i is known only at availability_sample_end=i+1. Retrospective overlap with C_i is duration bookkeeping and MUST NOT be interpreted as permission for an alarm at i/f. Actual alarms must keep causal end timestamps and future detector-window latency. No matching, alarm generation, threshold or clinical abstention policy is introduced here. All-unavailable events remain in the original event count; they cannot be excluded to inflate sensitivity. Any final informative-duration secondary analysis must retain and report primary full-record denominators.

Implementation availability_audit.audit_availability joins exact original events with ready/invalid masks; per-event output preserves both ready and unavailable overlap. Memory/loop implementation is a reference finite-record primitive, not yet optimized whole-cohort execution. No real signal, QC threshold or FIR choice has been selected or evaluated.

Independent controls:6 groups,1364 exhaustive cell-count cases across event/readiness masks for lengths1..5, exact fractional-boundary overlap, fully unavailable events, and integration with a synthetic RecordStream reset/invalid/warmup fixture. Three malformed/nonboolean/truncated/contradictory mask cases reject. Prior recording-time/causal-stream/timely checks rerun. No new real data or annotation access. Mathematical partition proof is separate from finite controls.
