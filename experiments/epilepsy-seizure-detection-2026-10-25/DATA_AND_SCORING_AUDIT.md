## Pinned scoring audit and counterexample
Primary framework: https://epilepsybenchmarks.com/framework/ . Actual upstream implementation: esl-epfl/timescoring commit 426f8d2b77974641dc9db71884e0812b249ba93b, package metadata version 0.0.7; scoring.py blob 4af14c16f8b7929f07a2882f3ea9806260710187, annotations.py dbe647781e562fd3381ca9d2ac027b359062529c. Vendored unchanged under its MIT license (vendor/LICENSE.timescoring); NumPy 2.3.5 used locally. No upstream contribution/contact.

Actual EventScoring operates at 10 Hz; default tolerances are 30 seconds before / 60 after, merges BOTH references and hypotheses across gaps <90 seconds, then splits BOTH at 300 seconds. Thus its reference denominator counts transformed events; it is not always the number of original clinical seizures. It uses overlap, not one-to-one bipartite assignment. Its false alarm denominator is total annotated recording time, not non-seizure time. No-reference sensitivity is NaN. The source webpage has wording mistakes (e.g. F1 geometric versus harmonic); actual pinned code and independent algebra govern this audit.

Concrete synthetic counterexample: reference [100,110) seconds, alarm [150,151), recording 300 seconds. Default scoring gives TP=1, FP=0 although the alarm is 40 seconds after seizure end. Changing tolerances and merge interval to zero gives TP=0, FP=1. Two references [100,110),[180,190) are one default reference scoring event. A 601-second reference is three units after splitting. These are documented scorer semantics, not claims of an upstream defect. A permissive benchmark score cannot establish timely detection.

Before clinical comparison freeze, specify BOTH standard SzCORE benchmark scoring and a complementary timely-detection endpoint with unmerged original clinical-event denominators, explicit onset deadline, alarm duration/time-in-alarm and no backdating of causal alarm timestamps. The strict-zero-tolerance profile tested here still splits long references and is NOT by itself that final endpoint. Keep time-to-first-real-alarm and missed events visible. Do not choose endpoint/tolerances from test results. Threshold selected for a candidate 90% validation sensitivity must not be readjusted on tests; 90% is a research choice, not a clinical norm.
