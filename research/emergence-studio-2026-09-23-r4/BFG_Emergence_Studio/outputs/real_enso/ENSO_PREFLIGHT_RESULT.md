The carrier models monthly sea-surface-temperature anomaly relative to a
month-of-year climatology estimated from development data only.

Its forecast geometry is deliberately simple:

- endpoint A: climatological zero anomaly / mean reversion,
- endpoint B: previous-month anomaly persistence,
- BFG: one selected closure/neutral feature controls the adaptive mixture,
- matched null: one ordinary window statistic controls the same two-coefficient
  adaptive mixture.
