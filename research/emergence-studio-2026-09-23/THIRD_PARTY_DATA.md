# External observations and attribution

The three CSV datasets under `BFG_Emergence_Studio/data/` are attributed in their accompanying provenance files to the statsmodels dataset collection. The upstream metadata checked on 23 September 2026 explicitly describes each underlying dataset as public domain:

- [Sunspots metadata](https://github.com/statsmodels/statsmodels/blob/main/statsmodels/datasets/sunspots/data.py): National Geophysical Data Center annual sunspot observations.
- [CO2 metadata](https://github.com/statsmodels/statsmodels/blob/main/statsmodels/datasets/co2/data.py): Mauna Loa atmospheric CO2 observations, with the Keeling and Whorf source citation.
- [ENSO metadata](https://github.com/statsmodels/statsmodels/blob/main/statsmodels/datasets/elnino/data.py): NOAA ERSST.V3B Nino 1+2 monthly sea surface temperatures.

This is an attribution record based on the named upstream metadata, not an assertion that BFG owns those observations. Preserve the bundled provenance JSON files and their source credits. The repository's all-rights-reserved notice does not restrict public-domain observations or claim ownership of third-party material. BFG-specific source code, written analysis and figures are distinct from the underlying observations, to the extent protected rights exist.
