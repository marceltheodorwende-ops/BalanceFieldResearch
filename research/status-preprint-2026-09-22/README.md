## Scientific status

This is a preprint, not peer reviewed. Finite conditional closure does not establish universal BFG closure. The next-load selection, general nonnormal update, and infinite-dimensional domain preservation remain unresolved as described in the seven-problem status table. Synthetic figures and external measured data are explicitly distinguished. A conventional RLC fit is not empirical confirmation of BFG; the direct free-circuit persistence assignment fails the required eligibility test.
