## Immediate sequence
1. Use S1–S3 of PROOF_CHAINS as the current correction to the selection argument. The historical package stays intact and the counterexample stays visible.
2. Seek an independently justified strict sign condition; if none is established, retain Neutral-Contrast Selection as DECLARED, not DERIVED.
3. Apply the same source-to-premise test to G1 and the transverse-response identity.
4. Continue the documented reciprocal-alpha self-consistency problem as a separate conditional mathematical question. Fixed-alpha sheet bounds do not resolve variable-alpha intervals.
5. Keep empirical tests of the declared finite candidate separate from historical necessity. Neither a positive nor negative dataset result automatically settles the logical source-derivation question.

## Stop rule for each proof attempt
Record source, assumptions, exact claim, argument and counterexample search. If a needed premise is not derived, stop the claim at a conditional theorem and list that premise explicitly. Do not report a universal impossibility merely because a bounded source search did not find a proof.

The three-rule audit does not require adopting an external physical equation. Standard spectral calculus, polarization and matrix inequalities are mathematical tools already used by BFG.
