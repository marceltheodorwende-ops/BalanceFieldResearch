## Concrete strengthening work

Start with a source-to-rule lemma ledger: list the older axioms actually used by each completion rule, derive each implication, and attempt an inequivalent completion satisfying the same axioms. For Neutral-Contrast Selection, the resolvent identity is already sourced; the missing step is why its positive spectral sector is the uniquely required selection. For intrinsic-Gram load and transverse recursion, prove uniqueness or retain them explicitly as additional rules.

Next address reciprocal-alpha self-consistency independently of the already narrower scalar inverse-fiber argument. Then treat coupled noncommuting continuum evolution and rank events with explicit admissibility hypotheses. Additional numerical runs can probe counterexamples, but cannot replace these existence and uniqueness arguments.

These sources strengthen traceability and clarify the proof target. The search does not justify announcing unconditional universal closure. No historical paper or negative result has been rewritten to make that announcement.
