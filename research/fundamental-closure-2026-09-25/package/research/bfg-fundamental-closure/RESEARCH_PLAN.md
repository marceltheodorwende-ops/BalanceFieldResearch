# Research Plan

## Current finite-law status

The living package already has a deterministic finite candidate **conditional on three
declared BFG-internal completion rules**:

1. intrinsic-Gram successor load;
2. Neutral-Contrast Selection;
3. neutral transverse recursion.

The historical source corpus alone does not uniquely force those rules.

## Immediate mathematical priorities

1. **Source-derivation audit of the three finite completions.**  
   Try to derive, constrain, or replace each completion from older BFG axioms without
   importing external physics.

2. **Reciprocal-alpha self-consistency.**  
   Decide whether a fixed readout can support an interval of reciprocal
   \(\alpha\)-values or whether every admissible full-persistent inverse fiber is finite.

3. **Full noncommuting same-rank continuum.**  
   Extend the positive two-channel/frozen constructions to simultaneous local evolution
   of \(Y,\rho_F,\rho_W,R_C\) without freezing the neutral geometry.

4. **Event consistency.**  
   Keep rank-changing Selection/R4/formation transitions as exact stratum events unless
   a new persistence semantics is explicitly adopted.

5. **Quantitative fixed-dimension memory bounds.**  
   The existence of a qualitative o-minimal bound \(B_d\) is known; obtain useful
   explicit bounds where possible.

## Physics program after the finite law is frozen

Only after the universal finite law and its continuum/event architecture are stable:

- derive candidate causal/relational geometry;
- derive a gravity-like effective sector;
- derive gauge/matter organization rather than importing a gauge group;
- derive probabilistic/quantum statistics rather than inserting a quantum equation;
- derive cosmological scaling/large-scale structure;
- identify dimensionless predictions and failure modes.

No sector is counted as “recovered” until the result is derived rather than fitted or
inserted.

## Stop conditions for a claim

A claim must be downgraded or rejected if it requires:

- sector-specific retuning;
- a hidden external physical law;
- a numerical tolerance presented as an exact law;
- an unproved uniqueness step;
- an unverifiable numerical result with no reproducible script;
- contradiction with the authoritative state contract or claim register.
