# Prior-Art and Novelty Boundary

## Known mathematics used in the package

The following mathematical tools/classes are treated as known mathematics, not new BFG
mathematics:

- spectral functional calculus;
- polar decomposition and partial isometries;
- Riesz spectral projectors;
- Lyapunov/power-bounded finite-dimensional stability facts;
- Schur/Hadamard multiplier channels;
- correlation matrices and Gram representations;
- Stinespring/Gram-type channel realizations;
- Schoenberg conditional-negative-type / infinite-divisibility criteria;
- principal-angle/Grassmannian same-rank projector geometry;
- Böttcher conjugacy for a superattracting scalar map;
- inverse and implicit function theorems;
- Whitney simple-fold normal form;
- o-minimality of the real exponential field and uniform finiteness consequences.

When a BFG-derived object falls into one of these classes, the BFG-specific derivation
of the object may be novel, but standard consequences of the class remain prior
mathematics.

## BFG-specific structures investigated here

Examples include:

- the exact neutral pair \(C_N(Y),B_N(Y)\) and neutral contrast;
- reciprocal cross-fed BFG load construction;
- two-density separation of formation and inherited witness;
- Selection-first ordering in the living finite candidate;
- the specific three declared finite completion rules;
- BFG-specific use of R4 transport, emergent simple-negative formation seed, and
  neutral transverse recursion;
- the BFG two-channel angle representation of the frozen Schur multiplier;
- the package's phase-anchored continuum and branch-memory constructions;
- carrier-dimension monotonicity of the current cross-fed finite candidate.

## Novelty discipline

Do not claim novelty merely because:

- a standard theorem is applied to BFG data;
- a known channel class is rediscovered from BFG notation;
- a numerical witness has not been seen before;
- a completion rule is new to this package.

Novelty requires a clearly identified BFG-specific statement beyond the standard
mathematical theorem used to prove it.

## External physics boundary

GR, QFT, the Standard Model, Schrödinger dynamics, Yang–Mills theory, and other physical
theories may be used later as comparison/falsification targets or derived-limit
benchmarks. They are not admissible as fundamental input laws to the BFG-only master
update.
