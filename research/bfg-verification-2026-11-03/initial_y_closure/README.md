# Direct closure check of the initial-Y derivation

**4 November 2026.** The existing explicit candidate has now been evaluated directly, its assumptions traced to their stated source status, and its exact selection domain checked. The algebraic formula is complete **under the existing proposed L0-NC rule**. A derivation of that rule's source-neutral identifications from the earlier formation assumptions is still not established. The proposal is not adopted here.

This report consolidates the direct question rather than treating more auxiliary calculations as evidence that the foundational derivation must eventually succeed. It introduces no new paper, physical law, fitted parameter or change to the published preprint or finite event.

## 1. The formula already exists, with a precise conditional proof

The [September 30 proposal](../../level0-neutral-capacity-source-law-2026-09-30/PROPOSED_LEVEL0_AMENDMENT.md) explicitly labels itself a proposed new source law. It does not claim historical derivation from the original formation section. Its formula is

    H_*=K_0-lambda_0 I-2 lambda_0 Pi_0 > 0,
    A=H_*^(-1/2) aleph_0 H_*^(-1/2),
    Y=A^2.

Here K_0=c_0+aleph_0-D_cap, lambda_0<0 is its simple isolated ground eigenvalue, and Pi_0 its rank-one spectral projector. The first equation is derived from the source quartic at its real formed minimum. The proposed part identifies the neutral tangent carrier and prescribes the two completed-square blocks:

    B_mix=-aleph_0,      M_intrinsic=H_*.

Writing the joint assessment as

    J(D,eta)=||D-L eta||_(H_*)^2/2+||eta||_M^2/2,

the mixed block is -H_*L. Thus the proposed identification gives L=H_*^(-1)aleph_0 and M=H_*. The already established neutral-elimination formula then gives

    Y=H_*^(1/2)L M^(-1)L*H_*^(1/2)
     =H_*^(-1/2)aleph_0 H_*^(-1)aleph_0 H_*^(-1/2)
     =(H_*^(-1/2)aleph_0 H_*^(-1/2))^2.

No commutation of aleph_0 and H_* is needed. This is exact inside the specified quadratic tangent assessment. It does not turn the parent quartic into a globally quadratic function.

The current source was fetched again and remains explicitly proposed. Its Git blob and commit are pinned in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json); this is not a reinterpretation based on a remembered draft. The [September 29 bridge](../../completion-boundary-2026-09-29/LEVEL0_NEUTRAL_TANGENT_BRIDGE.md) likewise distinguishes supplied formation, supplied neutral completion and the extra carrier identification.

## 3. An exact source-level criterion for full selection

For any self-adjoint aleph_0 and H_*>0, the candidate A is self-adjoint. Functional calculus and congruence give

    0<=Y<I
      iff -I<A<I
      iff -H_*<aleph_0<H_*.

The inequalities are strict positive-definite operator inequalities. If aleph_0>=0, full strict selection is equivalent to H_*-aleph_0>0. This condition is about **all source directions**. The finite selector can still retain a proper subspace when it fails; failure of this whole-carrier criterion is not automatically failure of the complete event.

More generally the exact neutral selector is

    Q_N=1_[0,1)(Y)=1_(-1,1)(A).

Its spectral calculation can be expressed by the generalized eigenproblem aleph_0 v=a H_*v, with whitened eigenvector H_*^(1/2)v. No extra empirical threshold is introduced. A=0 directions survive this selector but may still fail the complementary-load gate.

These are new exact domain checks of the existing candidate, not a source proof selecting the candidate itself.

## 4. Direct evaluation of the existing two-dimensional example

The proposal's already published comparison data are

    K_0=diag(-1,2),
    aleph_0=[[1,1/4],[1/4,1]],
    c_0=diag(0,3),
    D_cap=[[2,1/4],[1/4,2]].

They satisfy K_0=c_0+aleph_0-D_cap. The formed Hessian is H_*=diag(2,3), and the candidate evaluates exactly to

    A=[[1/2,1/(4 sqrt(6))],[1/(4 sqrt(6)),1/3]],
    Y=[[25/96,5/(24 sqrt(6))],[5/(24 sqrt(6)),35/288]].

Both H_*-aleph_0 and H_*+aleph_0 are positive definite, so every source direction is selected. The off-diagonal Y is nonzero and [K_0,Y] is nonzero. This confirms that the proposed formula does not merely force a scalar response.

For the **supplied test choice** rho_F=rho_W=P=|e_1><e_1|, let mu=<e_1,Y e_1>=25/96. Since full selection holds, direct metric projection gives

    Lambda_C=1/(1+mu)=96/121,
    Lambda_B=mu^2/(1+mu)=625/11616,
    alpha=Lambda_B/(Lambda_C+Lambda_B)=625/9841.

Both loads are positive. The exact stacked Gram has rank two and positive determinant. The next formation operator is -I on that two-dimensional active target; inherited support has rank one and its complement supplies seed trace 1. A fresh call to the preserved event confirms the rank, seed, formation trace 2, next K and Gram trace. The choice of initial witness and P in this test is not claimed as a new origin derivation; the proposal addresses the tangent response, not every initial role.

## 5. The proposal's scalar boundary also matters

For its scalar example K_0=-1, aleph_0=2 and H_*=2, the same formula gives A=1 and Y=1 exactly. The November finite selector requires y<1 and therefore excludes this scalar direction. The preserved implementation terminates at selection, matching the exact boundary.

This does not invalidate the positive neutral completion or its conditional formula. It demonstrates that computing a positive Y and guaranteeing a successful finite event are different claims. Neither the scalar boundary nor successful two-dimensional input is empirical validation of natural realization.

## 6. Closure status and the remaining research question

The candidate's calculation, normalization and strict-selection domain are now explicit. It would be misleading to say that only more matrix manipulation is needed.
