# Foundation-to-event derivation audit

3 November 2026. Follow-up inside the existing BFG verification stage. This is a research record, not a new paper, new physical theory or revised publication.

## Question and bounded answer

Does the precise finite event in Section 12 of the published preprint follow uniquely from the historical structural principles, without additional constitutive identifications?

**Established:** the explicit structural packet P below is insufficient to select the exact successor load. A parameter-free alternative preserves the stated packet but gives a different successor on the same one-dimensional state. **Not established:** a formal independence theorem for the conjunction of every sentence in every historical BFG manuscript. Those texts contain different state spaces, proposed dynamics, unspecified functionals and later amendments, not one agreed formal axiom system. Failure to locate a proof is not itself an impossibility theorem.

The existing September [completion-boundary dossier](../../completion-boundary-2026-09-29/README.md) already records relative G/S/R nonuniqueness. This audit is not presented as discovery of that general issue. It connects it directly to the November five-tuple event and revisits specific earlier source claims. The [September neutral-capacity amendment](../../level0-neutral-capacity-source-law-2026-09-30/README.md) is explicitly a later additional rule and cannot silently be used as an original axiom.

## Structural packet P used in the countermodel

P includes the finite five-tuple state and absorbing terminals; the exact input neutral pair C=(I+Y)^-1, B=Y(I+Y)^-1; positive complementary neutral responses; the same input selector, density loads, cross-weights and ambient analysis A; Hermitian kernel, positive formation, normalized witness and orthogonal persistence; no ambient-dimension increase; the same polar transport, K compression and seed gate on that input; unitary covariance and a strictly positive successor load below I on success. There is **no** premise in P identifying the successor load with exactly Sigma^2. That equality is the target choice under examination, not an assumption to be smuggled into its derivation.

P is a precisely stated mathematical packet extracted for this test. It is not claimed to encode neutral admissibility, proto-modes, consciousness or every historical BFG condition completely. The absence of a full bridge from those terms to this packet remains an explicit open obligation.

## Proposition F1: nonuniqueness relative to P

Let U0 be the published event. For each successful output `(K+,rhoF+,rhoW+,S,P+)`, define the logical witness U1 to keep all components except to replace S by `h(S)=S(I+S)^-1`. Keep every original terminal terminal. This is a test construction only; U1 is not adopted as BFG or proposed as a law of nature.

**Proof.** On success the ambient-event theorem gives 0<S<I. Spectral functional calculus gives eigenvalues s/(1+s) in (0,1/2), so positivity, rank and the strict upper bound are retained. All other output types and input-dependent gates are unchanged. The next input can use the same declared neutral algebra because its load remains admissible. Functional calculus commutes with unitary conjugation, so quotient covariance is preserved. No adjustable coefficient has been introduced. On the one-dimensional input `K=-1, rhoF=rhoW=P=1, Y=1/2`, the published load is 8/45 while U1 gives 8/53. One-dimensional loads with distinct values cannot be unitarily equivalent. Hence P does not determine the exact successor load. QED.

If `Y+=Sigma^2` is included as a defining axiom, U1 is excluded immediately. There is no contradiction with the uniqueness of the explicitly defined event. The question then moves to whether that defining axiom has an independent derivation from earlier premises.

## Source-local checks

1. **Early balance ratio.** In the April 2025 sources, the normalized ratio fixes an instantaneous relation. With I=I0=Ebind=1 and Eexp=Phi, both Phi(t)=1+exp(-t)/2 and Phi(t)=1+exp(-2t)/2 satisfy it and share their initial state but differ in derivative. They also satisfy the product-rule definition of information feedback. This proves nonuniqueness for those identities alone, not for the full 2025 proposed meta-equations. Those additional equations must be treated as separate premises and justified on their own terms.
2. **Level-0 stability, Section 22.** Set all three positive weights to one, admissibility failure and contrast collapse to zero, recurrence support to one, and Phi_n=2+1/(n+1). The printed stability functional is strictly decreasing, bounded below, and has a nonzero balance-error limit. Thus the listed numerical descent conditions alone do not force Phi_n to one. The phrase 'up to bounded admissible fluctuations' does not specify a numerical tolerance; if interpreted as allowing any bounded deviation, it cannot establish a definite approach to balance. This is a precise gap in that local argument, not a refutation of all BFG stability results.
3. **Process time, Section 17.** The printed increments are differences of F=Phi I, so their accumulated sum is exactly F(X_n)-F(X_0). A closed sequence with F values 1,2,1 has nonzero changes and total process measure zero. The definition alone does not give a monotone clock. No new time rule is inserted to fix it.
