## 4. What no-choice permits us to conclude

The assignments cannot all equal the unique fundamental origin rule. The stated structural packet does not choose one of them. A further source implication must exclude the alternatives or a missing origin datum must be supplied. Calling one assignment 'minimal' supplies a criterion only if that criterion is already justified.

The no-choice discipline therefore prevents promoting a convenient assignment into a proved consequence. It does not turn underdetermination of an initializer into a mathematical failure of a fully specified event, and it does not authorize returning the event's terminal object before an input has been supplied. Initial data and dynamical laws have different roles: a deterministic theory can legitimately admit many initial states. Deriving one unique origin is a stronger research objective than proving deterministic evolution from given data.

The earlier origin subset remains consistent. Its equality W_0=Pi_0 and the least-support choice P_0=Pi_0 are conditional boundary restrictions unless their representation/support premises are established. There is no contradiction in using them as an explicitly conditional example; the prohibited step is claiming that the original premises have already forced them.
