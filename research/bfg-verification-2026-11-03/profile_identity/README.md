## 2. Exact criterion for the identification

Let K be self-adjoint on a finite Hilbert space of dimension at least two, with simple lowest eigenvalue lambda_0, projector Pi_0, and gap Delta=lambda_1-lambda_0>0. For every positive trace-one W, the following conditions are equivalent:

1. `W=Pi_0`.
2. `W=Pi_0 W Pi_0`.
3. `tr((I-Pi_0)W)=0`.
4. `tr(KW)=lambda_0`.
5. `W <= c rho_F` for some finite c>0, where `rho_F=(-lambda_0)Pi_0` and lambda_0<0.

Condition 5 is an operator-order condition, not an extra density normalization.

**Proof.** Conditions 1 and 2 are equivalent because a normalized operator supported on a rank-one line is its projector. For 3, `tr(QW)=||Q W^(1/2)||_F^2=0`, where Q=I-Pi_0. Thus QW^(1/2)=0 and W=Pi_0 W Pi_0; the converse is immediate. The spectral inequality `K-lambda_0 I >= Delta Q` implies

```math
\operatorname{tr}(KW)-\lambda_0
\ge \Delta\operatorname{tr}(QW)\ge0.
```

Consequently 4 implies 3, and 1 implies 4. Under 5, every vector v orthogonal to the formed line has `0<=v*Wv<=c v*rho_F v=0`; positivity implies Wv=0, yielding 2. Conversely, 1 gives 5 with `c=1/(-lambda_0)`. QED.

In dimension one a positive trace-one W is already the identity projector; no gap is needed. If the ground eigenvalue is degenerate, condition 4 fixes only support inside the whole ground eigenspace and not a unique pure witness. These boundaries are substantive.

Thus a source premise requiring the *same ground-minimizing formation role for the witness* would close the identification. The theorem does not establish that this premise is already part of BFG, and it is not adopted as a new rule here. The quadratic value is used as an algebraic formation diagnostic, not identified with measured physical energy.

## 3. Quantitative version

Define the nonnegative excess epsilon=tr(KW)-lambda_0 and p=tr(Pi_0 W). Then

```math
0\le1-p\le\min(1,\epsilon/\Delta),
\qquad
\|W-\Pi_0\|_F^2\le2\min(1,\epsilon/\Delta).
```

For the second bound, positivity and trace one give tr(W^2)<=1. Hence
`||W-Pi_0||_F^2=tr(W^2)+1-2p<=2(1-p)`. This provides a rigorous diagnostic if an independently justified representation supplies epsilon; it does not determine epsilon or add an evolution law. The result concerns witness alignment at fixed K, not the sensitivity of K's eigenvectors to perturbing K.

For `K=diag(-2,1)` and the pure tilted witness generated by `(4/5,3/5)`, epsilon/Delta=9/25 and the squared Frobenius distance is 18/25: the bound is attained. For a mixed witness the inequality can be strict.
