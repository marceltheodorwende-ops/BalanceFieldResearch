## Self-check and failure conditions

Checked symbol domains and stages, rectangular transport dimensions, positivity of losses, normalization factors, and rank-one seed trace. The product of partial isometries was deliberately treated as a contraction, not assumed to remain a partial isometry. Euclidean Gram and G-weighted load accounting remain distinct. No full-orbit claims are inferred from a stage counterexample or a frozen-Y recurrence.

The derivation must be revised if the executable update places Selection differently, if a seed has different rank/weight, or if the source's Schur formula is used outside its stated stratum. Terminal events have no ordinary successor density; the identities apply only to nonterminal events. No physical tolerance or new completion function was introduced.

A full independent audit of the preprint, implementation equivalence and continuum existence was not performed. These are remaining tasks, not implied successes. Computational checks were not run; proofs and exact arithmetic are the evidence for this stage.

Recommendation: retain the finite balance results; resolve the stage-input convention in the implementation before expanding to a continuum generator or labelling an observable energy. Preserve both counterexamples as part of the research record.
