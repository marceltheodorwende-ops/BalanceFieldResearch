The preprint proposes a neutral-capacity source rule for a finite formed-branch tangent sector. It proves the resulting channel, Gram load, and resolvent under that rule, gives non-uniqueness counterexamples under the earlier data, and states a conditional finite successor contract. Its new source rule and successor identifications are declared constitutive choices; the document does not claim that they follow uniquely from the historical Level-0 equation. The mathematical references support the methods used, not independent empirical verification of BFG. Natural-system mediation and universal irreducible emergence remain open.
