# Multiscale Neural Organization and Conscious Closure in the Balance Field Framework

Marcel Theodor Wende · Preprint · Version 1.0 · 1 October 2026

An ontological synthesis with conditional mathematics and testable EEG hypotheses.

## Paper

Read the [original preprint](BFG_Multiscale_Neural_Organization_and_Conscious_Closure_Preprint_2026-10-01.pdf). This PDF is preserved byte for byte.

The paper connects level-resolved organization, common-versus-contrast response modes and an exact identifiability control in a six-state linear response model. It distinguishes ontological interpretation, conditional mathematical results and prospective empirical hypotheses. It does not establish a sufficient condition for consciousness, a physically identified neural mediator or a validated clinical biomarker.

## Reproducibility status

The original accompanying script and archive were not supplied. A separately labelled [simulation supplement](simulation_supplement/README.md) now includes Table 3 as CSV and a new reconstruction of the six cases from the published equations and parameters, with 60-step trajectories and verification results. All six rows agree with the paper's printed precision. This reconstruction does not recover original source files, manuscript hashes or figure-generation code. Download the separate simulation ZIP in this folder.

The manuscript reports AI assistance and identifies itself as a preprint draft. Its declarations and scientific limitations remain as stated in the PDF.
