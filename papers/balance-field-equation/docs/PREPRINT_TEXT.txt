Abstract
This preprint specifies the Balance–Field Equation (BFG) as a finite, stratified recursive operator dynamics on
unitary equivalence classes. Its declared state is =(K,Ξ ρF,ρW,Y,P). Spectral selection, canonical polar transport,
an endogenous positive metric, formation-dependent loads, an ambient reduced singular-value decomposition,
R4 realignment, formation inheritance and a rank-one seed rule define a unique successor class or a named
terminal outcome. The complete analysis operator acts on the ambient input space, not only on the selected
carrier. This choice has restrictive consequences: R4 is isometric on the selected carrier, its witness mass is
exactly one, every nonterminal successor satisfies 0<Y_+<I, and the ambient dimension cannot increase.
Subsequent nonterminal events preserve witness mass. Geometric self feedback is explicit and produces strict
damping on a scalar control branch, but neutral kernel and formation directions prevent a universal strict full-
state contraction claim. The manuscript gives typed differentials, proof assumptions, event-time bookkeeping
and independently reproducible numerical controls. Physical information, seconds, spacetime and gravity,
quantum and particle observables, atoms, molecules, biology and experience require additional specified
measurement bridges. These transitions are presented in dependency order and remain open where the finite
kernel does not determine them. Computed 3D figures demonstrate declared mathematical families; physical-
sector illustrations do not constitute empirical validation.
Keywords: Balance–Field Equation; recursive dynamics; polar decomposition; singular-value decomposition;
viability; endogenous geometry; quantum bridge; recursive closure; witness persistence; stratified dynamical
systems.
1 Introduction
A candidate world formula must do more than compress many concepts into one symbolic expression. It must
specify a state space, an evolution law, admissibility conditions, equivalence relations, regime boundaries, and
a map from internal variables to measurable quantities. The present construction takes this requirement
literally. The BFG is therefore formulated as a recursive operator system whose central object is an orbit of a
finite state under a stratified update. The use of polar decomposition and singular-value structure places the
mathematical core in a standard matrix-analytic setting [4–8], while viability and Lyapunov concepts provide
the appropriate language for constrained persistence [9].
Three co-authored precursor lines are retained as direct BFG provenance. The Level-0 foundation with Thomas
W. Loker motivates a pre-informational admissibility layer [1]. The recursive conjugate-closure work with
Luke Kenneth Casson Leighton motivates mediated conjugate recursion [2]. The no-supervenience bridge with
Alfredo Pereira Jr. motivates the later distinction between functional closure and phenomenal interpretation
[3]. No other BFG manuscript is included in the bibliography.

the same algorithm on the newly generated active space.

80 Frequently Asked Technical Questions
80.1 Is the BFG one equation or many
It is one quotient evolution equation with an explicit factorization. The sub-operators are comparable to the
factors of a numerical integrator or a structured map: they specify how the single successor is computed.
80.2 Why not use a scalar state
The five-component operator tuple is the declared state of this construction. It resolves spectra, magnitude,
normalized witness and moving supports explicitly. This is not a proof that every alternative representation
requires five components.

80.3 Why is the successor dimension allowed to change
Because the reduced SVD treats only active positive singular directions as successor coordinates. Rank is
therefore a dynamical output. Fixed-dimensional padding would introduce null directions whose physical and
seed status would be arbitrary.
80.4 Does gauge quotienting hide physical information
No information invariant under allowed coordinate changes is removed. Spectra, traces, positivity, rank,
masses, and all simultaneously conjugated relations are preserved. The quotient removes only coordinate
labels.
80.5 Is m_tot a Born probability
Not by definition. It is a mathematically normalized two-stage witness survival probability inside the kernel. A
physical Born/instrument interpretation is a separate bridge hypothesis.
80.6 Is G spacetime
No. G is positive definite and internal. Spacetime is Lorentzian and requires a separate typed bridge.
80.7 Does scalar damping prove stability of the universe
No. It proves one scalar inequality on a declared branch. Full-state stability requires control of every
endogenous derivative path and must account for neutral directions.
80.8 Does the Standard Model follow already
Not numerically. The BFG supplies a reconstruction program, but masses, charges, couplings, mixing
parameters, and scattering observables must be generated by a fixed ΠSM and tested.
80.9 Does witness mean consciousness
No. Witness is a mathematical persistence role. World/Self and phenomenal experience require additional
operational and empirical bridges.
80.10 What would make the BFG a physical world equation
A finite set of frozen measurement bridges that convert independently prepared BFG states into successful
quantitative predictions across time, geometry, quantum/particle, and higher-level sectors without post-hoc
remapping.
