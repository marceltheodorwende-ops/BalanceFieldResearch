## Evaluation rule

Freeze the preparation map, observable map, instruments, reference scales, horizon and loss criterion before evaluating held-out observations. Compare against established models and preserve negative results. A numerical illustration supports a calculation in its stated model; it does not prove a theorem or independently validate the physical identification.

## Mediator convention

For an amplitude mediator $N_A\succeq0$, $M=N_A^{1/2}$. For a direct mediator $N_B$, the general expression is $N_BRN_B^\dagger$. At $R=I$ these give $N_A$ and $N_BN_B^\dagger$, respectively. They agree when $N_B=N_A^{1/2}$; writing $N_B^2$ additionally requires self-adjointness.
