## Scope

The preprint classifies trajectories of a specified autonomous finite BFG event, including finite completion, load collapse and formation-kernel convergence under its stated assumptions. It develops an explicit three-node RC model and distinguishes excluded fixed-clock amplitude correspondences from a designed one-mode event-clock realization. It also treats DC versus oscillatory power and derives thermal-residual drift, variance and fixed-time coverage bounds.

The event clock implements a prescribed recursion; it is not independent empirical confirmation of a natural law. The paper reports conditional mathematical results, analytical calculations and reproducible computational controls. It reports no laboratory experiment and does not establish a universal physical BFG law. General physical state correspondence, rank-changing robustness and infinite-dimensional extensions remain open obligations.

## Reproducibility
